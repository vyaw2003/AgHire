FAQ

Q: How do I create a listing?
A: Tap "Create New Listing", fill in the required fields, add images, and submit.

Q: Where is AgHire available?
A: AgHire currently supports listings and users in Australia, New Zealand, the United States and Canada.

Q: How can I advertise?
A: AgHire is accepting advertisers. Contact us for details.

Q: How can I pause my listings?

  • To pause your listing: Login to the app → My Listings → Select Listing and Pause the listing directly.

Q: How can I delete my account and listings?

  • To delete your account:
    • Open the app.
    • Go to Main → Account Management → Delete My Account.
    • Confirm deletion. Your account and listings will be permanently removed.
  • Alternatively, you can send a request for an admin to delete your account on your behalf by emailing us.
